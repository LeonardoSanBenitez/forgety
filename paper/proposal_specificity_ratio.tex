From these, we derive 39 \textit{InterferencePerEntity} metrics using a simple
combinatorial aggregation procedure (described in \cref{ap:metrics}).
%
Additionally, we define one further \textit{MetricInterferencePerEntity} that does not
aggregate \textit{InterferencePerEntityPair} values but instead directly compares the
visual representation of the forgotten entity before and after unlearning:
%
$$m_{e,\text{spec}}(e, u) = \frac{d_\text{self}(e, u)}{d_\text{others}(e, u)}$$
%
where $d_\text{self}(e, u)$ is the cosine distance between the DINOv2 mean embedding of
entity $e$ under the unlearned model and under the original model,
and $d_\text{others}(e, u)$ is the mean such distance computed over all other entities
prompted in the same unlearning run.
A ratio $\gg 1$ indicates that the forgotten entity's visual representation shifted
substantially more than the background drift induced by the unlearning procedure,
characteristic of a specific unlearning effect.
A ratio $\approx 1$ indicates that the method caused equal shifts across all entities
(non-specific).
A ratio $< 1$ indicates that the entity was less affected than background, implying
that the unlearning procedure did not target it effectively.
%
For \ac{spare} on the \textit{people} task, the mean ratio across 20 entities is $1.79$
(range $[1.67, 1.94]$); for \ac{uce}, the mean is $1.37$ (range $[0.96, 1.66]$).
