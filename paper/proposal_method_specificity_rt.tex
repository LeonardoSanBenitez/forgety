{\bf MethodSpecificity}. Distribution of a single \textit{MetricInterferencePerEntity}
across multiple \textit{unlearning methods}.
\underline{Arguments}: $m$, $t$, $m_e$, list of $u$.
\underline{Result}: per-method mean, median, and standard deviation of
$\{m_e(e, u)\}_{e \in t}$; box plot.
\underline{Interpretation}: quantitative; methods can be ranked directly by their mean
or median value, with the direction of improvement ($\uparrow$ or $\downarrow$) inherited
from the $m_e$ definition.
%
For example, invoking \textit{MethodSpecificity} with parameters $m=\text{SD}$,
$t=\text{people}$, $m_e=\text{EmbeddingSpecificityRatio}$ and
$u \in \{\text{\ac{spare}}, \text{\ac{uce}}\}$ yields a mean ratio of
$1.79$ (median $1.81$) for \ac{spare} and $1.37$ (median $1.36$) for \ac{uce},
confirming that \ac{spare} produces a substantially more entity-specific unlearning
effect in the \textit{people} task.
